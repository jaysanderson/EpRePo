# Persona 9 - Prof Ivers, clinical trialist and pharmacologist (ASM safety)

## 1. Persona and session

**Who:** Prof Ivers, clinical trialist and pharmacologist. Comes for RCT evidence (lacosamide adjunctive in PGTCS, fenfluramine, cenobamate, perampanel, brivaracetam), valproate and pregnancy registers with dose-specific major congenital malformation (MCM) rates, dose-response, retention, systematic reviews and meta-analyses, trial design (endpoints, common data elements). Secondary surfaces: Evidence review intent, the Agentic page, export.

**What I set out to do:** re-test the CLINICIAN-REVIEW-2 P0-2 valproate question and verify every number; pull primary endpoints and safety figures from the trials the corpus holds; ask for synthesis (placebo response, outcome sets) and see whether the portal says what the corpus lacks; run Deep research; export an answer and check it carries usable citations.

**Run:** 4 Sept 2026, 12:20 to 13:05 local, against `http://localhost:8787/t/eprepo` (981 resources), shared with nine other agents.

**Counts:** 16 Ask questions (14 routed via `/route`, 2 forced to `review`, 1 Deep research), 6 document-chat questions across 3 resources, 12 API searches plus 3 UI searches, 8 resources inspected via API and 3 opened in the browser, 2 headless passes (1440 px and 390 px, light plus a dark palette), 1 export captured. Every number quoted in an answer below was string-matched against `GET /resources/:id/content` of the cited resource; the raw streams and content files are in this directory (`q*-raw.sse`, `q*-final.md`, `content-*.json`, `shots/`).

## 2. Verdict in five sentences

The numbers are finally right: every figure in fourteen answered questions and six document-chat answers was found verbatim in a cited paper, the valproate dose thresholds that were fabricated in review 2 now come from the Vajda register paper that contains them, and the three questions the corpus cannot answer were declined cleanly rather than bluffed. What I cannot yet do is verify claim by claim, because the platform sprays every citation onto one sentence ([1][2][3][4][5][6] on a responder-rate sentence, two of them Dravet papers and one a protocol), and one answer stitched a 2005 Australian register's 1400 mg threshold into "the 2024 JAMA Neurology study" as if EURAP had reported it. The router still misfiles trialist questions: "synthesise ... sample size" went to Supplementary data and returned ten irrelevant supplements as its sources, and "core outcome set" went to Supplementary data and told me the corpus has no core outcome set when the EPSET protocol is sitting in it. The Kind labels are not usable for my job (171 "Randomised Controlled Trial" resources including a 2005 review, a 2008 mouse study and an open-label extension), and a merchandised title reads "Glucosamide". I would use it again for a first pass and I would trust the declines, but I would not put an answer in front of a grant panel without opening the PDF, and the citation binding and routing need fixing before a clinician pilot.

## 8. Feature wishes

1. **Per-claim citations with a hover excerpt** - I check numbers one at a time; a marker per number that opens the exact passage would replace my grep.
2. **A "study design" facet I can trust** (RCT, open-label extension, pooled/observational, registry, review, protocol) - it is the first filter a trialist applies.
3. **Numbers table on Evidence review** - when the answer quotes effect sizes, tabulate drug, dose, endpoint, value, CI, n and source paper.
4. **Register/cohort provenance line** - when a figure comes from a registry, state the register, year, and n in the sentence ("Australian Pregnancy Register 2005, n=172").
5. **Export with a real reference list** - authors, journal, year, DOI, and the passage quoted, so the .doc stands on its own in a grant appendix.
6. **Author search and an author page** - "Vajda" should return the register papers; "Vossler" should not return bexicaserin.
7. **Ingest tables** for text-only articles (EURAP Table 3) or link the PMC PDF, so dose bands are answerable.
8. **Decline with nearest matches** - when nothing answers the question, list the three closest papers by title so I know what the portal does hold.
