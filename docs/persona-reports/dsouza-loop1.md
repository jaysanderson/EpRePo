## 2. Verdict as Prof D'Souza, and score

When it is right it is very good: the SUDEP-with-lamotrigine answer gave me my own aHR 0.56 (95% CI 0.31 to 1.01, P = 0.054) and the 16-year follow-up with a working link to the paper, and the rituximab answer reproduced HR 0.11, 28% relapse and the six-monthly dosing argument exactly as we wrote them. But I cannot put this in front of a registrar yet, because on three of my own studies it told me things that are not in the papers, with citation markers attached: BREATHS became a 98-participant trial against "standard medical care" (it is 220 against Befriending), UMPIRE became a retrospective 15-patient ECoG series (it is a prospective first-in-human study of 26), and PERMIT and the first-seizure PRO paper were declared absent from the collection when both are in it. The cause is visible in the interface: any question that asks for a number is routed to a "Supplementary data" configuration that reads only supplements and peer-review files, so the primary paper is excluded and the model improvises from whatever supplement is nearest, and the same routing empties the results list for a plain search like "rituximab anti-NMDAR". Add a Kind facet that labels my observational cohorts, a rat study and a diabetes GWAS as Randomised Controlled Trials, a "figures checked" badge that flags correct figures and never shows in the interface, and 12 to 27 seconds to the first word, and the honest answer is that the portal currently makes me do the checking the software should have done.

Impact score: **4 / 10**. Open P0: 2. Open P1: 9.

## 5. The single change that would move the score most

**Stop the `data` intent from excluding the primary papers.** Six of sixteen questions (Q1, Q4, Q9, Q10, Q12, Q15) went to a supplements-only configuration because they asked for a number, and that one routing decision produced both P0s, two false "not in the collection" statements, the wrong-subgroup answer, the funding-declaration "answer" for my name, and an empty results list for "rituximab anti-NMDAR". Concretely: make `data` retrieve articles plus supplements (or run both and merge); restrict the classifier's `data` choice to queries that mention tables, supplementary material, data sheets or peer review; skip routing entirely when `/search` reports a lookup match; and force any resource whose title matches a study acronym in the query into the source set. Re-asking under General already proves the rest of the pipeline returns the right figures (Q4b, Compare configurations). This alone moves me from 4 to about 6.

Next three:
1. **Kind labels** (D1-06): rule-first study-design classification with a confidence gate; hide the badge otherwise. The facet is currently a liability for the epidemiology and trial-design jobs.
2. **Evidence card = the passage with the figure, and a real audit badge** (D1-08, D1-12, D1-13): choose the card passage by matching the cited figure, pass its page to the reader and scroll the PDF, fix the matcher for enumerations, show "N figures checked" on every answer.
3. **Latency** (D1-09, D1-07, D1-18): rules and cache before the classifier, retrieval in parallel with classification, lookup and search results shown without routing. Target under 5 s to first token on clinic questions and instant author and identifier results.
