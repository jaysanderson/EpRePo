# Persona evaluation p4 - Dr Rahimi, neuroimaging scientist

## 1. Persona and session

**Who.** Dr Rahimi, MRI physicist turned clinical neuroimaging researcher. Comes to the portal for
quantitative MRI and EEG-fMRI methods, diffusion and volumetric biomarkers, glymphatic imaging,
tau PET and the epilepsy-neurodegeneration link, machine-learning lesion detection, and the
acquisition parameters, sample sizes and tables in methods sections.

**What I set out to do.** Pull exact acquisition parameters and table values out of the imaging
papers (7T MRS, ENIGMA volumetrics, MELD lesion detection, EpiBioS4Rx preclinical MRI), test the
Supplementary data intent on real data sheets, test honesty on three things the corpus probably
does not hold (7T MP2RAGE T1 in hippocampal sclerosis, tau PET tracers in epilepsy, DTI-ALPS in
TLE), and run the Extraction Lab on a table-dense data sheet.

**Run.** 4 Sept 2026, roughly 11:40 to 13:10 AEST against http://localhost:8787/t/eprepo,
shared with nine other agents (frequent HTTP 429 on /route and /ask, see Errors log).

**Counts.** 20 asks (13 distinct Ask questions, 3 of them re-asked with a forced `general`
intent to isolate a routing defect, plus 4 document-chat questions on two resources), 11 API
searches plus 3 through the UI, 16 resources read through `/resources/:id/content`, 3 resource
pages opened in the browser, 2 Extraction Lab profiles and 1 compare job, headless visual passes
at 1440 px and 390 px.

**Method note (own error, corrected).** My first SSE reader decoded byte by byte and dropped
multi-byte characters, so early transcripts showed `12.010.81 mM` and `Cramr-Rao`. Re-reading
the same stream with `curl -N` shows `12.01±0.81` and `Cramér-Rao` intact. No finding is
based on that artefact; every quote below is from the raw stream or the UI.

## 2. Verdict in five sentences

For the questions where retrieval reached the right paper, the portal read methods sections and
supplementary tables back to me exactly, with TR, TE, voxel volume, multiband factor, coefficients
of variation and Cohen's d all matching the source PDFs, and the resource page with the PDF
alongside "Chat with this document" is the workflow I actually want. I would not yet put it in
front of a student or a grant panel unaided, because in one of thirteen questions it invented a
sensitivity and specificity for the MELD classifier from an unrelated neurofilament data sheet
and put a citation marker on it, and in three more it told me the corpus did not cover things
that it plainly does. The cause is visible and fixable: the intent classifier sends almost any
question containing a number, a parameter or a metric to the supplement-only "Supplementary
data" configuration, which hides the articles and leaves the generator with 0.01-relevance
scraps. Search is weaker than Ask for my vocabulary: "EEG-fMRI" is routed to an exact-lookup
keyword search that ranks a rodent data-element report first on a reference-list snippet, and a
PMC identifier that is in the resource metadata returns twelve unrelated papers. I would use it
again today for document chat and for pulling parameters out of a paper I have already found, and
I would use the whole thing once the data-intent routing and the low-relevance answer guard are
fixed.

## 3. What is good

- **Exact parameter recall from methods sections.** "What TR, TE and voxel volume were used for
  the STEAM MRS acquisition..." (forced `general`) returned "TR ... 8500 ms, except for
  participant 5 on day 1, where it was 9300 ms due to reaching a SAR limit. The TE was 6 ms, and
  the voxel volume was 20 20 20 mm, which is 8 mL [1][2]" - every value verified in
  `af4a298fd7e0405f9132a57023497b99` page 1. The multiband EPI question returned "1.6 mm
  isotropic, a temporal resolution of 800 ms, an acceleration factor of 12 (2 by parallel and
  6 by multiband), and a total of 300 volumes [1]" - verified in `97da7fa3a90a48989fd3c5cdc9db8098`.
- **Supplementary table numbers, when the data intent is the right one.** "mean NAA concentration
  on day 1 scan 1 ... coefficient of variation for glutamine" returned "12.01±0.81 mM ...
  8.23%±6.32% [1]" citing `c0954aa7f97f4d31b3e8a92c2b78348f`; both match Supplementary Table 1.
  The ENIGMA 3T-only Cohen's d for left hippocampus, "-0.34 [1]", matches Supplementary Table 10
  row "Hippocampus (LH) -0.34 -0.35 0.07 ..." in `d3aacd086dc443ba8dd3ed83eb59f3f1`.
- **Document chat is precise on a two-page data sheet.** "For participant 3, what are the four
  raw water-scaled GABA values" returned 0.822, 0.813, 0.913, 0.957 as a list, matching row 3 of
  Supplementary Table 2; "mean inositol concentration on Day 2 Scan 2" returned 7.59 mM
  (table: 7.59±0.90).
- **Honest decline when the corpus really lacks it.** The 7T MP2RAGE T1-in-hippocampal-sclerosis
  question returned the standard "does not hold enough relevant material" refusal with zero
  sources and zero citations, which is the correct outcome.
- **Large-cohort numbers are right.** ENIGMA cerebellum: "1,602 people with epilepsy and 1,022
  healthy controls ... Cohen's d ... (TLE-HS-L) was 0.56 [1]"; ENIGMA AI: "SVM ... 76% ... DL ...
  74% [1]"; 7T GluCEST stroke: "19 patients ... 1.97 mmol/L, with the ipsilateral concentration
  being lower [1]"; EpiBioS4Rx (forced general): "43 rodents (32 TBI and 11 sham) ... 41 rodents
  (31 TBI and 10 sham) ... 43 rodents (33 TBI and 10 sham) [1] ... 4.7 to 9.4 T [2]". All
  verified against the cited resources.
- **The figure audit exists and fired.** The fabricated MELD answer carried "*Figures in this
  answer that do not appear in the cited passages: 1276. Verify against the sources before
  relying on them.*" It did not catch the 82% and 93%, but the mechanism is there.
- **Resource page.** Title, PDF badge, topic chip, authors, journal, year, DOI link, "View
  original source", an in-page PDF reader (page N of 9, fit, zoom, print, download), a
  collapsible EXTRACTED TEXT panel, "Chat with this document" with three sensible suggested
  questions, a summary and key takeaways. The supplement page renders Supplementary Table 1 in
  the PDF viewer with columns intact. At 390 px the same page stacks cleanly, no horizontal
  overflow (`scrollWidth 390`), controls wrap to two rows.
- **Search chrome.** Hybrid/Semantic/Keyword toggle, topic and kind facets with counts, a
  match-strength filter with an explanation, "Watch this search", "Ask about these results" and
  "Summarise these results" are all what a researcher expects. No console errors on any page.
- **Extraction Lab profiler** is quick and correct: the 2-page data sheet profiled in 0.76 s as
  `class: tables`, `tableRowsPerPage 20.5`, `imageOnlyPages 1`, `source: poppler`; the 9-page
  article as `prose`, 4,869 chars/page, `dictionaryHitRate 0.993`. The Lab copy ("The extraction
  layer decides what this portal can ever know...") and the three method cards (Default,
  table-aware, visual-transcribe with its transcription rule) are understandable to an imaging
  scientist without reading the docs.
- **Routing chips** ("Supplementary data · rule", "Exact lookup · rule") make the routing
  decision visible, which is exactly what let me diagnose the defects below.
