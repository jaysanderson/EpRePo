## 6. The single change that would move the score most

**Make the figure audit a gate on the text, not a footnote under it, and make it accurate.** All four P0s (D2-01 to D2-04) and D2-06 share one shape: a number that is not in the cited passage (or is in it about a different outcome or population) is still printed as a cited fact, and the only defence is an italic line listing bare numbers - which on the same day also listed BREATHS's correct 220 and 110, "Thirteen" and "11 p.m." Concretely: (1) after `done`, any sentence whose figure the matcher cannot find in its cited paragraph is either re-generated against the cited paragraphs only or dropped, and a figure sentence with no marker is never shown; (2) the matcher handles number words, units, table cells, clock times and outcome nouns so it stops crying wolf (D2-07); (3) the denominator helper only pairs an n with a proportion in the same sentence. Platform: ask with `answer_json_schema` so each statement carries `{claim, figure, paragraph_id}` and the audit checks a paragraph id rather than a sentence window; use `citation_threshold` to drop uncited claims; pin named resources with `resource_filters`. The document chat proves the rest of the stack is right - the LGI1 paper answered N01's question correctly in document scope while the corpus-wide ask got it wrong. This moves me from 6 to about 8.

Next three:
1. **Cross-paper and briefing content from the papers' own results** (D2-02, D2-03, D2-06, D2-16): ground comparisons and briefings in Results/Abstract paragraphs and the DA-generated key takeaways and stat fields, one retrieval per named drug or study, and exclude Introduction/Discussion paragraphs; keep the secondary-citation boundary sentence on every surface (Search inline answer too, D2-14).
2. **Pinned paper depth** (D2-05, D2-08): when a question names a paper or study, read more of it (document-scoped ask or `full_resource`) before saying the paper lacks something; answer partially when it holds the n but not the outcome.
3. **Coherent trust signal and timing** (D2-15, D2-17, D2-12): audit-led confidence only, platform groundedness never raises it, a "checking figures" state between `done` and `audit`, and a marker on every figure sentence.
