# Persona 10 report - Dr Baptiste, neuropsychologist and epidemiologist

## 1. Persona and session

**Who:** Dr Baptiste, neuropsychologist and epidemiologist. Interests: cognitive and mood outcomes in epilepsy, quality of life trajectories after a first seizure, SUDEP risk and mortality, employment and driving, epidemiology of drug-resistant epilepsy. Secondary surfaces: Library facets and sort, the Graph page, a 390 px mobile sweep.

**Set out to do:** re-test the first-seizure-and-driving question that CLINICIAN-REVIEW-2 flagged as P0-3; ask the questions I would ask of an Australian epilepsy corpus on QoL, mood, SUDEP, employment and drug resistance; test three questions the corpus almost certainly cannot answer; exercise every Library sort and facet combination; judge the Graph; measure the phone layout.

**Run:** 4 Sept 2026, 12:22 to 13:05 (about 43 minutes) against `http://localhost:8787/t/eprepo` (981 resources: 655 articles, 273 supplements, 53 media). Shared server with nine other agents; the ask limiter (20 per minute per IP) returned HTTP 429 on 7 of 13 first attempts, each retried once after 25 s (environment note, see Errors log).

**Counts:** 13 Ask questions (each routed then asked with the routed intent, raw streams saved as `Q01.sse` to `Q13.sse`), 1 Ask repeated through the browser UI, 6 document-chat questions across 3 resources, 10 searches, 15 catalog/facet API calls, 5 resource records opened via API and 3 in the browser, 2 headless passes (1440 px and 390 px) plus a third pass for the sources panel, PDF link and Library facet clicks. Every number, drug, gene and percentage in a cited answer was grepped against `GET /resources/:id/content`.

**Dark mode:** the app has no viewer-facing dark mode. `apps/web/src/lib/theme.ts` derives `colorScheme` only from a tenant-level `branding.paletteId` chosen in the admin Appearance panel, and there is no `prefers-color-scheme` rule in `apps/web/src/styles.css`. The eprepo tenant config carries no `paletteId`, so the portal is light-only for every visitor. Switching the tenant palette would have changed the shared server for the other nine agents, so I did not. All visual checks below are light mode; the dark-mode half of the brief is not testable without an admin change (recorded as P10-20).

## 2. Verdict in five sentences

For my own subject matter the portal is genuinely useful: the first-seizure QoL trajectory paper, the anxiety and depression scoping review, the Australian Epilepsy Project burden study, the psychiatric comorbidity mortality cohort and the Nightscales lamotrigine SUDEP case-control were each found, summarised accurately and cited with figures I could verify word for word (196 participants, HADS cut-offs 8-10 and 11-21, SMR 3.6 versus 2.5, aHR 0.56 with 95% CI 0.31-1.01, 52.0% versus 76.7% and 72.5% employment). I would use it again for literature triage and I would let a student use document chat, which was six for six on verified figures. I would not put an Ask answer in front of a patient or a grant panel unmodified, because the driving question is still broken: through the browser it told me the recurrence risk is "between 21% and 45% within the first two years" with seven citation markers, and not one of those seven papers contains that figure or a word about first-seizure driving rules, while a small amber triangle was the only hint that anything was wrong. Citation binding is unreliable more generally: in the SUDEP and post-ictal psychosis answers the bracket numbers point at COVID-19 surveys and a psychoses-of-epilepsy genetics paper for claims that actually sit in a different cited paper, so a reader who clicks [3] to check a number finds nothing. The Library cannot sort by publication year, its Kind labels are wrong often enough that I stopped trusting them ("Randomised Controlled Trial" on a cohort study, a case-control study, a natural history study and a 2008 mouse paper), and the Graph page is populated with amoxicillin, omeprazole, skin tests and a "26-year-old woman", which would end a demo in front of an epidemiologist.

## 3. What is good

- **Q02 (QoL trajectories after first seizure, Evidence review):** every claim verified in Foster 2026 (`9cb1d5d7`): 196 participants, EQ-5D-5L and QOLIE-31, improvement at 6 and 12 months, absenteeism and presenteeism reductions, informal care reduction in the acute symptomatic group, "anxiety and depressive symptomatology remained unchanged across all groups". Structured, hedged, 2 sources, 2 citations, 6.8 s to first token.
- **Q03 (SUDEP risk factors and lamotrigine, Clinical decision):** aHR 0.56 (95% CI 0.31-1.01, P = 0.054) and aHR 0.82 (95% CI 0.40-1.68, P = 0.588) verbatim in Nightscales 2023 (`020e1d7c`), the FDA warning context and the European population study sentence also in that paper, and the closing "Verify against current prescribing information before acting" is the right safety line.
- **Q04 (psychiatric comorbidity and mortality, General):** SMR 3.6 versus 2.5 and "No single psychiatric disorder by itself conferred increased mortality" both verbatim in `453cb4c1`.

This is exactly the paper I wanted and the answer reads like a competent abstract.

- **Q09 (verbal memory and hippocampal volume):** "Left hippocampal volume is the strongest predictor of verbal memory performance" and "rhinal cortex volumes were not significantly associated with either" verbatim in Sitoh 2026 (`55ceb633`).
- **Q10 (Austroads seizure-free period):** clean decline, "This portal's content does not hold enough relevant material to answer this confidently", no invented interval. Correct.
- **Q13 (post-ictal psychosis and suicide):** 6-7% prevalence on video-EEG, 23% violence and 7% suicide attempts, the 26-year-old woman with frontal lobe epilepsy, and the clustering / brain injury / intellectual disability risk factors are all in `195649f1`.
- **Document chat, 6 of 6 verified:** 77 of 196 (39%) follow-up completers; HADS 8-10 borderline and 11-21 abnormal; 101 SUDEP cases, follow-up to 16 years with the 1 October 2018 and 5 April 2021 census dates; TCS frequency adjusted; 5.9% formal care (median 20 h, IQR 12-55) and 17.7% informal care (median 16 h, IQR 7-101); EQ-5D-5L and QOLIE-31 with n = 172. "Austroads" returns 0 results with a clean empty state rather than a false match.
- **Resource page (wide and 390 px):** title, kind and topic chips, authors, journal, year, DOI link, "View original source", an embedded PDF viewer with page controls, chat with suggested questions, summary and key takeaways. On the phone it stacks cleanly with no horizontal scroll.
- **Mobile layout:** `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` was exactly 390 on Ask, Search, Library, a resource page, the Graph and an entity page at the default font and at a 22 px root font. The Library switches to a single-column list with a "Filters (1)" button; the Search facets stack below the results.
- **Bylines and curated titles:** none of the 24 default Library cards was a raw filename (24 of 24 were articles, Format pre-selected to Articles); supplements read "Supplementary material 1: <paper>", videos "Video 3: <paper>".

## 6. Coverage notes

1. **Fitness-to-drive standards (Austroads, private licence seizure-free intervals):** not in the corpus. Q10 declined honestly. Q01 did not: it answered the driving part from a cannabis-prescribing paper's generic bullet, and in the browser run attached a recurrence figure to seven papers that do not contain it (P10-01, P10-02).
2. **First-seizure recurrence risk as a primary study:** the corpus holds the AAN figure only as a quotation inside a 2017 post-traumatic epilepsy management review (`3f5e25de`, "(21–45%) (Level A)"). The McIntosh first-seizure-clinic paper (`2eee9a51`) is in the corpus but was never retrieved for Q01. The portal never said the figure was a quoted guideline.
3. **Epilepsy incidence in Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Australians:** not in the corpus beyond a sensitivity note in the ten-year burden projection (`83bc89d3`, "alternate inputs for Aboriginal and Torres Island people ... Notes S6"). Q11 said "specific incidence rates are not detailed", which is honest, but added an uncited sentence about emergency admissions.
4. **NDDI-E screening outcomes:** the instrument is described (sensitivity 90% in the scoping review) but no implementation study exists; Q12 said so and labelled its inference as inference. Acceptable.
5. **SUDEP:** well covered (Nightscales 2023, polygenic risk 2025, Kv11.1 biomarker, medullary pathology, EEG/ECG risk model) and the answers used them.
6. **Cognition and mood:** well covered for first seizure, autoimmune encephalitis, Dravet (ENVISION) and TLE memory; nothing on neuropsychological outcomes after surgery beyond the ATL long-term paper, and nothing on cognitive side effects of specific ASMs except the 2013 review. The portal did not claim otherwise.
7. **Employment:** one strong Australian paper (Marquina 2024) plus the Foster trajectory paper; nothing on vocational rehabilitation. The Q06 answer flagged the absence of absenteeism data "for the Australian cohort" even though Foster 2026 has it (minor under-claim).

## 8. Feature wishes

1. **Sort by publication year and a year-range facet** - an epidemiologist reads newest first and "Newest added" is meaningless after a bulk import.
2. **Study-design facet that is actually correct** (cohort, case-control, cross-sectional, RCT, case report, review, protocol, preclinical) - I filter by design before I read anything.
3. **Inline "unsupported figure" strike-through** in Ask - the amber icon is invisible; a number no cited passage contains should be visibly marked in the sentence.
4. **"Quoted guideline" badge** when a figure is a cited paper quoting another source (the AAN 21-45% inside a PTE review) - otherwise the portal implies primary evidence.
5. **Outcome-instrument entity pages** (HADS, QOLIE-31, NDDI-E, WPAI, EQ-5D-5L) listing every paper that used them - this is how I would actually browse.
6. **A real epilepsy knowledge graph** with genes, syndromes, ASMs and outcomes, and hide the page until it exists.
7. **Compare two papers side by side** (cohort size, instruments, follow-up, effect sizes) from the Library, for the meta-analysis mindset.
8. **Export answers with the verified-figure audit attached** so a grant panel can see which numbers were checked against text.
