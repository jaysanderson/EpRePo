## 6. The single change that would move the score most

**Make the gate's "is this figure in the cited paper for this claim" test recall-correct on the paper's own sentences, and make every corrector prove its candidate the same way.** Fourteen of the loop's removals (D5-01), two of its false cohort verdicts (D5-02), the three wrong "denominator corrections" (D5-03) and the seven wrong quantities under a clean badge (D5-04) are one problem seen from four sides: the matcher decides on figure tokens and nearby words rather than on the sentence or table row the figure lives in. Concretely: (1) locate the figure inside the cited paper's own text first (Results, abstract, tables, graphical abstract), take the sentence or table row that contains it, and pass the claim if that sentence shares the claim's outcome noun or the routing entity - "80% of EDs ... during the sleep period", "24 participants completed", "Number deceased 60 87" under a "psychiatric disorder" column header all pass; "the placebo 50% responder rate was 50%" fails because the row that carries 1,674 says 395 (23.6%); (2) let the denominator corrector and the cohort guard use the same located sentence, so a candidate n must sit in that sentence (3/28 stays, "56 (46.3)" is never chosen) and a pinned paper is by definition the question's cohort; (3) when the located sentence is in the pinned paper but its outcome differs (TFA's 33.6% all-cause), remove at that turn and say which outcome the paper attaches the figure to. Normalise "⋅" and "·" with the other glyphs. Platform: ask for `citations: true` and `rag_strategies: [{"name":"neighbouring_paragraphs","before":1,"after":1}]` so the cited paragraph and its table neighbours are the audit's text, and use `/find` with `resource_filters` on the pinned paper (the DA `keyTakeaways` and `quotesOfInterest` fields already hold "Four subjects responded with > 50% seizure reduction" and "Half were assigned female sex" as first-hand sentences to match against). This takes me from 7 to 8 or 9 on its own, because it converts today's honest deletions back into cited numbers without reopening the loop 4 P0s.

Next three:
1. **Session context on every follow-up** (D5-06, D4-07 partly): carry the previous turn's cited resource ids as the pin on any context turn, and read the pinned paper's tables before declining ("that study", "the JME cohort", "how does that compare").
2. **The table turn** (D5-05, D4-06): strip fences, blank a failing cell instead of deleting the row, generate reformat-only turns from the session's verified sentences with a larger token budget, and gate the mislabels at the turn that produced them (TFA) so the table is never the first place they are caught.
